About me

Postdoctoral Associate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at MIT.

I’m currently a postdoctoral researcher in the lab of Otto Cordero exploring fundamental questions in microbial ecology while discovering new ways to engineer diverse microbiomes. My previous research experience was as one of the first graduate students in Aditya Kunjapur’s lab at University of Delaware and as an undergraduate researcher in Matt DeLisa’s lab at Cornell University. I am excited about the potential for synthetic biology to help address challenges in human health, agriculture and the environment, as long as we do so responsibly and effectively. I’m a native of Baltimore, MD with a love of the outdoors, arts and crafts, and microbiology podcasts.
